## Blocking evidence gaps

- **validation:** Accepted live-business process, data and reconciliation baselines are not established by source analysis. Local repository tests are a separate evidence scope.
- **components:** Installed SAP software components, add-ons, business functions and support levels are not present in source exports.
- **target:** No captured modernization intent is present.
- **runtime:** Activation, customizing, clients, effective roles and runtime usage are not observed.
- **readiness:** SAP readiness, simplification, ATC and product compatibility findings have not been imported.
- **landscape:** The source product, edition and release are not established by the export.
- **target:** The target product, route or release is not fully specified.

## Downstream contract

Every SAP specialist playbook consumes `.cognidev/sap-modernization/assessment-bundle.json` using schema `sap-modernization-assessment/v1`. It must preserve fact states and evidence references and must not convert an unknown into a passing assumption.

This report is an assessment baseline. It does not execute a migration or establish product compatibility, business equivalence or cutover readiness.
